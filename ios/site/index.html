The gym timer you never have to look at.

Pick a workout, press start, put the phone down. Tiger buzzes on every step, keeps time with the screen locked, and logs the session to Apple Health.

Free. iOS 17 or later. Needs Apple's TestFlight app.

A buzz on every change

Work, rest, a new block and the last three seconds of a countdown each feel different, so you know what happened without looking.

Runs with the screen locked

Tones ducked over your music keep you on time in your pocket. The timer keeps going when the phone sleeps.

Your session on the Lock Screen

The exercise, the countdown and your load × reps in a Live Activity and the Dynamic Island. Tap Done, +15 s or Skip rest without unlocking.

Apple Health, both ways

Every finished session counts towards your rings. Your bodyweight and heart rate come back in, so the calories are measured, not guessed.

470+ workouts, or write your own

Circuits, strength days and EMOMs ready to run, with an editor for sets, loads and rests. Last time's weights carry over.

Up next on your home screen

A widget with the workout you'd do next and this week's count. One tap opens it.

One history, phone and web

Sign in with Apple or an email code and your sessions sync with tigerworkouts.com. Or skip it — no account needed to train.

Picks up where you stopped

If the app closes mid-workout, reopen it within six hours and the session carries on from the same step.
