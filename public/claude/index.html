TigerWorkouts for Claude

Coaching from your real training history, new workouts written straight into the app, and a guided timer in the chat.

What Claude can do with it

  • Read your profile and training history: the loads, reps and times you did.
  • Search your workouts, public workouts and a catalogue of about 480 (benchmarks, lifting programmes, NHS plans, follow-along videos).
  • Write new workouts into your account, or change your own. New workouts are private unless you ask for them to be public.
  • Act as your coach: workouts Claude writes for you arrive in the app as "New · made for you", under a coach name you choose. Rename or remove the AI coach any time under Me.
  • Run a workout now with a timer in the chat, and log the session when you finish.
  • Give you a link that opens a workout on tigerworkouts.com with no sign-in.

Connect

  1. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors and add TigerWorkouts. Before the directory listing is live, use Add custom connector with https://mcp.tigerworkouts.com/mcp.
  2. Sign in with your TigerWorkouts email (we send a 6-digit code), or tap "Start now, no account needed".
  3. Ask away. Claude asks before any tool that writes to your account.

Claude Code: claude mcp add --transport http tigerworkouts https://mcp.tigerworkouts.com/mcp, or the plugin, which adds coaching skills: /plugin marketplace add Holzherr/tigerworkouts then /plugin install tigerworkouts@tigerworkouts. More clients: mcp.tigerworkouts.com.

Tools

Read-only tools run without asking; tools that write ask you first.

  • Reads: get_profile (name, units, bodyweight, training maxes, AI coach), list_sessions and get_session (your training history), list_workouts and get_workout (yours, public and the catalogue), search_exercises, preview_workout_url (a no-sign-in link), start_workout (the timer in the chat).
  • Writes: create_workout, update_workout, log_session, set_ai_coach (the coach's name, or switch it off), claim_account (keep a "Start now" account with your email).

No account needed to start

"Start now" makes an account with no email. Anything made with it stays private, and it is deleted with everything in it 7 days after it was created. To keep it, ask Claude to "keep my TigerWorkouts account", give your email and click the link we send. Then sign in with that email on tigerworkouts.com or in the iPhone app and the same workouts are there.

Try asking

  • "Look at my last month in TigerWorkouts and tell me what to change."
  • "Write me a 30-minute upper-body session for tomorrow using my last loads."
  • "Find a 20-minute kettlebell workout and start it."
  • "How did this week compare with last week?"

Your data

Claude acts as you: it sees your own workouts and history plus public workouts, nothing of anyone else's. We do not receive your conversations. Disconnect any time from Claude's connector settings, and delete your account in the app (Me → Delete account). Details in the Privacy Policy.

Workouts and suggestions are not medical advice. Use your own judgement on loads and exercises, and stop if something hurts.
