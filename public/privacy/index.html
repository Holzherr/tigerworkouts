Privacy Policy

TigerWorkouts · last updated 10 October 2026

TigerWorkouts (the website at tigerworkouts.com and the iPhone app) is run by Brambruesch Ltd, United Kingdom. This page explains what we store about you and why.

Without an account

You can browse and run every workout without signing in. Your workouts, history and settings then stay on your device and are never sent to us.

With an account

If you sign in (email code, password, Google or Sign in with Apple), we store:

  • your email address and the name you choose;
  • the workouts and exercises you create;
  • your workout history: what you did, when, weights, reps and times;
  • your favourites and settings.

This syncs your data between the website and the app.

Apple Health

Apple Health is off until you turn it on in the app. When on, the app saves each finished workout to Health, and reads your bodyweight and the heart rate recorded during a workout. That heart rate and bodyweight are stored with your workout history so you can see them on the website too. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone except as described under "Coaches" below.

Sharing

  • Public workouts: if you mark a workout public, other users can see it along with your name.
  • Coaches: if you link your account to a coach, that coach can see your workout history. Either of you can end coaching at any time.
  • We don't sell your data and there is no advertising or third-party tracking in the website or the app.

Where it's stored

Account data is stored with Supabase, our database provider. The website is served through GitHub Pages and Cloudflare. These providers process data only to run the service.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app (Me → Delete account). This removes your account and everything stored with it. Data kept only on your device is removed when you delete the app or clear the site's data.
